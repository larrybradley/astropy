Improved the performance of the biweight functions:

- ``biweight_location``, ``biweight_scale``, ``biweight_midvariance``,
  and ``biweight_midcovariance`` are faster by a factor of about 1.3 to
  1.4 when ``M`` is not input, by not computing the median of the data
  twice.
- ``biweight_scale`` and ``biweight_midvariance`` are faster by a
  factor of about 1.3 by avoiding a slow fourth-power calculation.
- ``biweight_location``, ``biweight_scale``, and
  ``biweight_midvariance`` are faster by a factor of up to about 8 for
  ``numpy.ma.MaskedArray`` input, by computing medians with
  ``nanmedian`` instead of ``numpy.ma.median``.
